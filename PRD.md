## Product Requirements Document

### Historical Cyclone Exposure Assessment and Infrastructure Planning Platform

---

# 1. What We Are Building

The platform is a geospatial analysis and visualization system designed to transform historical cyclone information, environmental datasets, terrain characteristics, and infrastructure data into understandable exposure assessments and planning insights.

The initial geographical focus is Odisha, India.

The primary case study is Cyclone Fani (2019).

The platform combines historical cyclone information with geospatial and infrastructure datasets in order to provide an accessible, visual, and interactive understanding of how geographical regions and infrastructure may be exposed to cyclone-related hazards.

The system is intended for research, education, demonstration, and planning-oriented workflows.

It is not intended to function as an operational forecasting system.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Historical cyclone information is typically distributed across numerous independent sources including:

- Meteorological records
- Satellite observations
- Government reports
- Terrain datasets
- Infrastructure databases
- Geographic information systems

Understanding how a cyclone affected a specific region often requires significant technical expertise and multiple software platforms.

This complexity creates barriers for students, researchers, planners, and evaluators attempting to understand historical cyclone exposure.

The platform aims to provide a unified workflow that simplifies this process while preserving transparency and analytical value.

---

# 3. Vision

To create an accessible platform that allows users to explore historical cyclone events, visualize hazard distributions, understand infrastructure exposure, and evaluate historical site suitability through an intuitive geospatial interface.

---

# 4. Objectives

The platform will:

- Visualize historical cyclone events.
- Present geographical hazard information.
- Analyze potential infrastructure exposure.
- Provide interactive geospatial exploration tools.
- Support educational understanding of cyclone impacts.
- Demonstrate data-driven infrastructure planning workflows.
- Present results through maps, dashboards, and reports.

# 6. Core Capabilities

## Historical Cyclone Explorer

Users can explore historical cyclone events and associated contextual information.

Outputs:

- Event information
- Cyclone path
- Impact overview

---

## Hazard Visualization

Users can visualize estimated hazard zones generated from historical information.

Outputs:

- Hazard maps
- District analysis
- Severity indicators

---

## Exposure Assessment

Users can understand how infrastructure intersects with historical hazard zones.

Assets:

- Buildings
- Roads
- Settlements

Outputs:

- Exposure indicators
- Summary metrics
- Comparative insights

---

## Construction Suitability Assessment

Users can evaluate a proposed development location.

Inputs:

- Location
- Building category
- Building height

Outputs:

- Historical Exposure Index
- Flood Susceptibility Indicator
- Terrain Assessment
- Planning Guidance

---

## Reporting Dashboard

Users receive visual summaries through:

- Maps
- Charts
- Statistics
- Recommendations

---

# 7. MVP Scope

The initial implementation focuses on demonstration quality and presentation readiness.

Included:

- Landing page
- Dashboard
- Interactive maps
- Cyclone explorer
- Assessment workflow
- Methodology page

Excluded:

- Authentication
- User management
- Live backend services
- Operational data pipelines

The MVP must function entirely using local demonstration datasets.

---

# 8. Construction Assessment Philosophy

The assessment module exists to support planning-oriented exploration.

It evaluates historical environmental exposure indicators.

It does not predict:

- Structural failure
- Building collapse
- Construction approval decisions

The outputs should be interpreted as historical context rather than engineering conclusions.

---

# 9. Success Criteria

The MVP will be considered successful if users can:

- Understand the purpose of the platform within one minute.
- Interact with maps and visualizations.
- Explore cyclone information.
- View exposure metrics.
- Complete a location-based assessment workflow.
- Understand platform limitations.

---

# 10. Future Expansion

Potential future development includes:

---

# 11. Out of Scope

The following are explicitly outside the scope of this project:

- Weather forecasting
- Cyclone forecasting
- Early warning systems
- Building collapse prediction
- Structural engineering analysis
- Real-time emergency response
- Government decision automation

---

# 12. Guiding Principles

Historical Analysis Over Forecasting

The platform focuses on understanding historical events.

---

Exposure Assessment Over Damage Prediction

Outputs represent potential exposure rather than confirmed damage.

---

Visualization Over Complexity

Insights should be understandable without specialist GIS expertise.

---

Transparency Over Certainty

Assumptions and limitations should always be visible.

---

Educational Value Over Operational Deployment

The project prioritizes understanding, exploration, and demonstration.
